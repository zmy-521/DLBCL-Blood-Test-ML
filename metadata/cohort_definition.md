# Cohort definition and participant flow

Primary development: 65 DLBCL people with 285 paired panels and 430 distinct controls, total 495 people/715 rows. Primary external: 131 DLBCL people with 240 panels and 248 controls, total 379 people/488 rows.

Strict baseline: development 65 cases plus 430 controls; external 41 cases plus 248 controls. Case identity uses within-center strong source linkage and only nonconflicting secondary bridges. Names do not create identity links. Uncertain same-name multiple-entity records are excluded pending adjudication. Controls are investigator-attested unique individuals, including across centers; they are not asserted to have independently traceable LIS identities.

Use only source panels traced to originally included records. One chemistry and one CBC panel must exist for the same verified person and same day; reject missing or multiple same-day panel choices. A day with a single unique sampling date is preferred, otherwise a single test date is a flagged proxy. Same-day pairing does not imply the same draw.

All included case specimens were confirmed pretreatment by the investigator; structured treatment/steroid/diagnosis timestamps were unavailable. External cases include 102 sampling-date episodes and 138 test-date proxy episodes. Sixty-three external people have any sampling-based paired episode; only 41 meet reliable earliest chronology for strict baseline. Every included source panel must have a reliable sampling date for this strict eligibility decision, including source panels that did not become paired episodes. Baseline means earliest eligible episode within originally included records.

Reconstructed chemistry uses source item labels rather than obsolete wide-column positions. The original audit reconstructed 395 external chemistry records and repaired 126 prior misaligned records; those historical records and source audits are not distributed. The unsafe historical mixed-center file is excluded. Published aggregate counts cannot replace private row-level linkage evidence or prove source eligibility independently.

Repeated cases: 46 development, maximum 16 panels; 54 external, maximum 11. Panels are unweighted during training; patient performance averages panel probabilities. This difference in weighting is a limitation, not an independent-observation assumption for confidence intervals.
